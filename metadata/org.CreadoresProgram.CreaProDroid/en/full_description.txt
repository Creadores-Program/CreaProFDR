CreaProDroid is an artificial intelligence assistant designed specifically for older Android devices (versions 4.1 to 9). Powered by Google Gemini and Pollinations.ai technology, this assistant is optimized to offer a smooth and helpful experience on resource-constrained devices.
If this version is not compatible, you can try [CreaProPhone J2ME](https://github.com/Creadores-Program/CreaProPhone) with J2ME Loader, which extends compatibility back to Android 2.3.

## **Features**
- **AI Assistant**: Answers questions, performs tasks, and provides personalized assistance.
- **Legacy Android Compatibility**: Designed to run on devices running Android 4.1 through 9.
- **Voice Recognition**: Allows interaction using voice commands.
- **Image Generation**: Generates images based on user descriptions powered by Pollinations.ai.
- **App Launcher**: Can open installed applications on your device.
- **File Support**: Processes text files provided by the user.
- **User-Friendly Interface**: Intuitive and easy-to-use design.

## **Requirements**
- **Android Version**: 4.1 (Jelly Bean) to 9 (Pie).
- **Required Permissions**:
  - Internet access.
  - Read and write to external storage.
  - Microphone usage for voice recognition.


## **Usage**
1. **Initial Setup**:
   - Enter your Google Gemini API Key obtained from [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey).
   - Enter your name to personalize the experience.
2. **Interaction**:
   - Type or speak your questions or commands.
   - Use the microphone button for voice commands.
   - Attach files for processing.
3. **Additional Features**:
   - Generate images based on prompt descriptions.
   - Open installed applications on your device.
   - Clear chat history whenever you want.
   - AI Plugins support to add extended features like sending messages, making calls, and more.
   - Gemini Model selector.
